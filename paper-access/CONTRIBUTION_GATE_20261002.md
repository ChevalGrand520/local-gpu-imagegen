## Gate decision

Do not spend GPU time or increase fixed-case counts to lengthen the Access
draft. A standalone Access Research/Applied Research claim needs a new,
falsifiable value measure beyond the simple-stop and same-information-script
equivalences, plus a reviewer-inspectable evidence path for its core paired
result. No such measure is currently demonstrated. The seven-page length is
secondary to this evidence and contribution gap.

The current evidence fits a bounded tool/software account more closely:
product-integrated admission control, inspectable state separation and an
honest false-block cost. That route still requires a runnable distribution,
source/license decision, documentation and a privacy-reviewed evidence package.
Do not relabel software engineering integration as policy superiority.
