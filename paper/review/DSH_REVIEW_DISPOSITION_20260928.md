# Disposition of the 2026-09-28 DSH quality review

Status: author-side source and retained-record check; not a new experiment or independent replay of the Windows backend. The reviewer-supplied `DSH_REVIEW_20260928.md` is retained unmodified. This response addresses its claims, not the reviewer's intentions.

| Review point | Disposition | Basis and manuscript action |
|---|---|---|
| Same-run guard untested in Windows | **Accept, major evidence gap** | `f02_product_client.py` creates a new run per call and changes `seed=4100+call_index`; the guard applies to the original run. The abstract now says only synthetic CPU fixtures exercise the guard. No Windows guard benefit is claimed. A same-run real-backend study remains future work, not authorized by this review. |
| `execution_start=3` contradicts two distinct bindings | **Reject the claimed logical contradiction; retain the raw-evidence gap** | `_sanitize_oracle` sums `decision.raw_events` for each decision, whereas `ComfyUIEventOracle.raw_events` is its cumulative event list. For two decisions, lists `[A]` and `[A,B]` yield `1+2=3` aggregate entries while the two binding IDs for A and B remain distinct. F00 has one decision, F02 two, so the discrepancy being F02-only is consistent with this mechanism. The six-ID uniqueness check applies to retained binding IDs, not every aggregate event entry. The historical raw decision snapshots were not retained; the exact A/A+B decomposition, and absence of any unbound additional start, cannot be independently verified from the published extract. The manuscript now states both limits explicitly. |
| A third backend execution is established | **Not established** | The retained record has two proxy submissions and two bound execution instances per F02 case; the aggregate event count is not a unique-event count. A third unbound start cannot be ruled out from the sanitized extract, but neither is it observed as a third bound execution. It need not belong to a third run, and the paper never claims that changing seed prevents duplicate execution. Do not report a third execution or a deduplication failure. |
| Submission/execution units drift | **Partly accept** | The guard directly withholds a product submission. The CPU fixture observes a resulting worker-entry count, but this cannot be transferred to the Windows backend. The F02 CPU paragraph now names submission as the direct action and labels the execution difference synthetic. Table captions already define S/E separately. |
| CPU F02 single-stage tradeoff | **Accept with scope correction** | W3's retained synthetic trace has one worker start/finish and an artifact hash while `valid_completion=false`; B2 completes after two submissions. This is a meaningful controlled fixture result, not a real-backend causal estimate. The paper now explains that cost without calling it a deployment finding. |
| Windows derived extract lacks raw WebSocket/history snapshots | **Accept** | The audit checks internal consistency and retained inspection receipts. It cannot reconstruct full backend events, independently rehash private first-call outputs, or prove complete observation. This boundary was already in the paper and remains. |
| Font replacement is necessary | **Defer** | Tectonic's IEEEtran initialization emits `TU/ptm` shape warnings, while final PDF font streams are embedded. Nimbus Roman is a Times-compatible substitute; the CFP requires embedded fonts and size/leading, not a specific commercial Times file. Changing font packages solely to silence underfull/initialization notices is not a research-quality P0. Final PDF rendering still must be inspected. |

**Quality decision:** retain **Major Revision** for evidence structure, specifically the lack of real-backend same-run guard measurement and missing raw observer snapshots. Do not elevate the count discrepancy to a proven third execution. The present paper can be submitted only as a bounded Tool Description whose guard evidence is synthetic and whose Windows evidence is observation of retained bindings. A broader guard-efficacy or reliability claim requires a separately designed campaign with full per-decision event capture and original-run outcome tracking.

The DSH review also says the DSN 2027 CFP could not be verified. The [official DSN 2027 research-track call](https://dsn2027-berlin.github.io/call-for-contributions/) is available; its Tool category, page/abstract/anonymization rules, and dates are recorded in the submission-readiness file. The review's venue-information line is stale, not a reason to alter the scientific disposition.
