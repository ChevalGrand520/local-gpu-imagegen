The rewrite consolidates prose within the existing section structure. It does
not move adverse findings out of the main text. All citation occurrence keys,
figures and tables are preserved. Quantitative findings are not recalculated.
The before/after lexical count is reported in prose-compression-v014.json.
That count measures manuscript source compression, not external duplication.

Remaining decisions concern human authorship/declarations, final acceptance of
the PDF and artifact release. No submission-ready or independent certification
status is implied by this editing pass.
